The Transit

Real-time Hong Kong public transport arrivals

Core Features

Aesthetic, free to use with no ads. Real-time bus ETA, detailed routes, fares and schedules for Hong Kong buses. Support favorite routes collection, station map & street view, and custom commute reminders.

Full Transport Coverage

Centered on buses, including MTR, Light Rail, ferries, cross-border buses, Discovery Bay buses, Ma Wan buses, Hong Kong Tramways, and Peak Tram.

Minimal Design

Clean interface with light / dark mode and switchable black-white / colored bus route signs.

Privacy & Security

All transit data is sourced from official Hong Kong government and transport providers. The app connects directly to official servers without any third-party relay. No personal user data is collected or uploaded.
